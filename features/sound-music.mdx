Wild Guess & Match includes cheerful sound effects for taps, correct answers, coin collection, and victories, plus a soft background music loop. You can mute each independently from the top bar.

## Audio Controls

Two toggle buttons sit in the **top navigation bar**, giving you instant control over audio:

- **Sound button** (blue, speaker icon) — mutes and unmutes all sound effects, including tap clicks, correct and incorrect chimes, coin sounds, and the text-to-speech animal narration.
- **Music button** (purple, music note icon) — mutes and unmutes the soft background ambient music that plays while you explore and play.

Both settings are **remembered between sessions**. If you mute the music today, it will still be muted the next time you open the game — no need to adjust it again.

## Sound Effects List

| Sound | When you hear it |
|---|---|
| **Tap** | A short click whenever you select any button or card |
| **Correct** | An ascending chime when you answer correctly |
| **Incorrect** | A descending tone when your answer is wrong |
| **Match** | A bright tone when you find a matching pair |
| **Card Flip** | A light "whoosh" when flipping a card in the Match Game |
| **Coin** | A sparkle sound when coins are awarded |
| **Victory** | An upward fanfare when you complete a Match Game round or unlock the Drawing Studio |

## Animal Name Narration

The game speaks animal names aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech. In the **Guess Game**, you can tap the **Speak** button at any time to hear the question animal's name repeated clearly — great for younger players learning new words.

Narration runs through the same sound effects channel. If you mute sound using the Sound button, narration will be muted too.

<Tip>
  For the best learning experience, keep sounds on so children hear the animal names pronounced clearly!
</Tip>
