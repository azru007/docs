Coins are the reward currency in Wild Guess & Match. You earn them by playing games and answering correctly, and you can spend them to unlock the **Drawing Studio** — a creative canvas where you can paint your favourite animals. Your current coin total is always visible as a **yellow counter in the top header bar**, so you always know how close you are to your next reward.

## Earning Coins in the Guess Game

Every correct answer in the Guess Game earns you coins, and the longer your streak, the bigger the reward. A **streak** is how many correct answers you get in a row without making a mistake.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Base Reward — 10 coins">
    For every correct answer you give, you earn **10 coins**. This is the starting reward and applies from your very first answer all the way until your streak reaches 3.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Streak of 3+ — 12 coins">
    Once you answer **3 or more questions correctly in a row**, each correct answer earns you **12 coins**. The game also makes things a little more challenging at this point by showing you animals from the same category, so stay sharp!
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Streak of 5+ — 15 coins">
    Keep going and reach a streak of **5 or more** to earn **15 coins** per correct answer. The answer options also increase from 3 choices to 4, giving you a bit more to think about.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Streak of 10+ — 20 coins">
    Hit a streak of **10 or more** and you earn the maximum reward of **20 coins** for every correct answer. This is the hardest tier to maintain — a real achievement!
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrong Answer — Streak Resets">
    If you pick the wrong animal, your streak resets to **0** and the next correct answer goes back to the base reward of 10 coins. Don't worry — just start building your streak again!
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Earning Coins in the Match Game

In the Match Game, you earn **5 coins for every matched pair** you find. The total coins available in a round depends on the difficulty you choose before the game starts.

| Difficulty | Pairs to Match | Maximum Coins per Round |
|------------|---------------|------------------------|
| Easy       | 3 pairs        | 15 coins               |
| Medium     | 6 pairs        | 30 coins               |
| Hard       | 8 pairs        | 40 coins               |

Coins are awarded pair by pair as you find each match — you don't need to finish the whole board to keep what you've already earned during that round.

## Spending Coins: Unlock the Drawing Studio

The **Drawing Studio** is a creative painting canvas where you can draw, stamp animals, and save your artwork as posters. It costs **50 coins** to unlock.

On the Drawing Studio tab, you can see exactly how far along you are. A **progress bar** fills up as your coin balance grows, showing you the percentage of the way to 50 coins. When your balance reaches 50, the **Unlock** button becomes active — tap it to open the studio permanently.

Once you unlock the Drawing Studio, it stays unlocked for good. You can close the browser, come back days later, and the studio will still be there waiting for you.

<Note>
  Your coin balance is synced so it carries over every time you return to the game — as long as you are using the same browser and device where your profile was created. This means coins you earn today will still be there tomorrow.
</Note>
