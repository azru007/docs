Here are answers to the most common questions about Wild Guess & Match.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create a new profile?">
    A parent or guardian needs to answer a simple maths addition question first. Once correct, choose a nickname (up to 15 characters) and an animal avatar, then tap **Let's Play!**. See the [Create Profile](/getting-started/create-profile) guide for full steps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do I need to answer a maths question?">
    The maths gate is a **Parent Check** that prevents young children from accidentally creating multiple profiles or changing settings without a grown-up's help.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I earn more coins?">
    Play the **Guess Game** and **Match Game**. Correct answers earn coins — build a streak in the Guess Game for bonus coins up to **20 per correct answer**. See [Earning Coins](/getting-started/earning-coins) for the full breakdown.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I unlocked the Drawing Studio but it's showing as locked again.">
    This can happen if your saved game data was cleared. If you need to clear your browser data for this site, be aware that it will also reset your profile and coin balance. Try to avoid doing this unless you want to start fresh.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I play on a phone or tablet?">
    Yes! Wild Guess & Match works on phones, tablets, and desktop computers. The layout adapts automatically — a bottom navigation bar appears on small screens, and a sidebar appears on desktop.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does it work without internet?">
    The game supports offline play once it has been loaded at least once (Progressive Web App). The leaderboard requires an internet connection to show live rankings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the daily leaderboard reset?">
    Scores reset every day at **midnight UTC**. A countdown timer on the leaderboard page shows how much time is left before the next reset.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my nickname or avatar?">
    Currently, nicknames and avatars are set once during profile creation. To choose a different one, a parent can clear the browser's site data to start a fresh profile (note: this also resets coins and the Drawing Studio unlock).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There's no sound — how do I fix it?">
    Check that the **Sound button** (blue speaker icon) in the top bar is not muted. Also make sure your device volume is turned up and that your browser allows audio. On some devices, audio only starts after your first tap on the screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I see my rank on the leaderboard?">
    Your rank appears after you have submitted at least one score today (by completing a Guess Game answer or Match Game pair). If the leaderboard shows an error, check your internet connection and tap **Try Again**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
