The Drawing Studio is a creative canvas where kids can paint, erase, and stamp animal characters onto their artwork. Unlock it by spending **50 coins** earned from playing games — then it's yours to enjoy whenever you like!

## Unlocking the Studio

The Drawing Studio starts out locked behind a coin gate. Here's how you open it:

1. A **progress bar** on the studio screen shows how many coins you have collected toward the 50-coin goal. Watch it fill up as you play the Guess Game and Match Game.
2. Once your coin total reaches 50, the **Unlock for 50 Coins** button lights up and becomes tappable.
3. Tap the button to spend your 50 coins and permanently unlock the studio — the gates open right away!

<Note>
  The studio stays unlocked even if you close or refresh the game. You never have to unlock it twice.
</Note>

## Drawing Tools

The toolbox panel on the left side of the canvas gives you everything you need to create.

### Brush

Draw freehand on the canvas by tapping and dragging. Choose from **6 colours**:

| Colour Swatch | Name        |
|---------------|-------------|
| 🟤 Dark        | Near-black  |
| 🔴 Coral       | Coral / Red |
| 🔵 Sky Blue    | Sky blue    |
| 🟢 Green       | Leaf green  |
| 🟡 Yellow      | Bright yellow |
| 🟣 Purple      | Soft purple |

Tap any colour dot in the panel to switch to it instantly.

### Eraser

Select the Eraser tool and drag over any part of your drawing to rub it out. Adjust the **Size Slider** to erase a small detail or sweep away a large area.

### Animal Stamps

Select the Stamp tool to open the animal grid, then tap any animal to choose it as your stamp. Tap anywhere on the canvas to place it. Use the **Size Slider** to make stamps tiny or enormous before you place them.

### Size Slider

Drag the slider **left** for a thin brush or small stamp, and **right** for a thick brush or large stamp. The size range runs from a fine **2 px** line all the way up to a bold **40 px** stroke.

## Canvas Actions

| Button      | What It Does                                        |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Undo        | Reverses your last drawing action                   |
| Clear       | Wipes the entire canvas back to white               |
| Save Poster | Saves your current drawing to your personal Gallery |

Use **Undo** to step back one action at a time, or **Clear** when you want a completely fresh start.

## My Gallery

Every drawing you save with **Save Poster** appears as a polaroid-style thumbnail in your personal **Gallery** below the canvas. Scroll through your collection to admire your masterpieces. To remove a painting you no longer want, tap the red **Delete** button on its thumbnail.

<Tip>
  Try selecting the **Stamp** tool, picking your favourite animal, and tapping all over the canvas to make a colourful animal pattern poster — then save it to your Gallery and show it off!
</Tip>
