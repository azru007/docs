## How Animals Are Used in Games

Each animal in the library plays a role across the different activities in Wild Guess & Match:

- **Guess Game** — a random animal is shown as an image; you pick its name from a set of options. The game rotates through all animals in a shuffled order before repeating, so every session stays fresh.
- **Match Game** — animal images are paired with their **name**, **food**, **habitat**, or **special object** depending on the Match Style you choose. For example, you might match the Lion to its habitat (Savanna), or the Bee to its special object (Honey Jar).
- **Drawing Studio** — every animal is available as a stamp in the Drawing Studio toolbox, so you can decorate your artwork with any creature from the library.
- **Avatars** — several animals from the roster — including the lion, cow, monkey, and elephant — are available to choose as your player avatar during profile setup.

<Note>
  The animal library covers over 30 unique animals. The Guess Game rotates through all of them in a shuffled order before repeating, so every session is different.
</Note>
