The Match Game is a memory card game where you flip face-down cards to find matching pairs. Choose your difficulty and match style, then race against the clock!

## Setup: Choose Your Settings

Before the cards are dealt, you get to customise your game in two ways.

### Difficulty

Pick how many pairs you want to hunt for. More pairs means a bigger grid and a bigger challenge!

| Difficulty | Pairs | Cards | Grid   |
|------------|-------|-------|--------|
| Easy       | 3     | 6     | 3 × 2  |
| Medium     | 6     | 12    | 4 × 3  |
| Hard       | 8     | 16    | 4 × 4  |

### Match Style

Each animal card is paired with a partner card. Choose what that partner shows:

- **Name** — the animal's written name as text. Great for beginners!
- **Food** — a picture of what the animal eats.
- **Home** — a picture of the animal's natural habitat.
- **Toy** — the animal's special object.

Mixing a harder difficulty with a trickier match style (like **Hard + Home**) is the ultimate test of memory!

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the game">
    Tap **Play Game** after choosing your difficulty and match style. The grid appears with all cards face-down showing **?**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flip your first card">
    Tap any card to flip it over and reveal its content — an animal picture, a name, a food, or a habitat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flip your second card">
    Tap a second card. If both cards match, they **stay face-up and glow** — and you earn **5 coins**!
  </Step>
  <Step title="No match? No problem">
    If the two cards don't match, they both flip back face-down after a short pause. Pay close attention to where each card was — you'll want to remember for your next turn!
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the round">
    Match all pairs to finish the round. Your total time and coins won are shown on the summary screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Earning Coins

You earn **5 coins** for every pair you successfully match. The maximum coins available depends on the difficulty you chose:

| Difficulty | Pairs | Maximum Coins |
|------------|-------|---------------|
| Easy       | 3     | 15 coins      |
| Medium     | 6     | 30 coins      |
| Hard       | 8     | 40 coins      |

Every pair you find counts — even if you take a few wrong turns along the way!

## Tips for Beginners

<Tip>
  Start on **Easy** to get familiar with the animals, and choose **Name** as your match style so the pairs are straightforward. Once you're comfortable flipping and matching, bump up to **Medium** difficulty — then try **Food** or **Home** for an extra brain workout!
</Tip>

After completing a round, tap **Play Again** to replay with the same settings, or tap **Setup** to change your difficulty and match style and try a fresh challenge.
