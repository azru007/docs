The Guess Game shows you a large animal picture and asks **"Who is this?"** Choose the correct animal name from 3 or 4 answer buttons. Each correct answer earns coins — build a streak for bigger rewards!

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Meet your animal">
    The game shows a large animal image in the centre of the screen. Take a good look (and a good listen — the animal's name is spoken aloud)!
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your answer">
    Choose from 3 or 4 colourful name buttons below the image. Tap the one you think is correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the result">
    The correct answer highlights **green**. If you chose wrong, the correct option is revealed in green and your choice turns **red** so you know exactly what the right answer was.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep going">
    Tap **Next Animal** to move to the next round and keep your streak alive!
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Streaks and Coin Bonuses

Every correct answer adds one to your streak counter. The longer your streak, the more coins you earn per correct answer. A wrong answer resets your streak back to **0**.

| Streak       | Coins per Correct Answer |
|--------------|--------------------------|
| 1–2          | 10 coins                 |
| 3–4          | 12 coins                 |
| 5–9          | 15 coins                 |
| 10+          | 20 coins                 |

Keep answering correctly to climb the table and maximise your coin haul!

## Adaptive Difficulty

The Guess Game gets smarter as you get better:

- **Streak 3 or higher** — the wrong-answer buttons are no longer random. They are picked from the **same animal category** as the correct answer (for example, all farm animals), so you can no longer rule them out at a glance. Read the names carefully!
- **Streak 5 or higher** — the game adds a **fourth answer button**, giving you one extra option to sort through on every question.

The difficulty scales back down automatically whenever your streak resets, so each session always feels fair.

## Audio Feedback

The game speaks the animal's name aloud the moment you answer. Use the **🔊 Speak** button at the top of the screen any time you want to hear the question again before you tap.

Sound effects also play to celebrate correct answers and flag incorrect ones, giving you instant feedback without even looking at the colours.

<Tip>
  Listen carefully to the spoken name **before** you tap an answer button — hearing the word alongside the picture is one of the fastest ways to learn a new animal!
</Tip>
