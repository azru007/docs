Welcome to **Wild Guess & Match** — a free-to-play browser game where you learn to recognise and name animals through four interactive activities. Every activity you play earns you coins, so the more you explore, the more you collect!

## What's in the Game

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Guess Game" icon="magnifying-glass" href="/games/guess-game">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Match Game" icon="grid-2" href="/games/match-game">

## Who Is It For?

Wild Guess & Match is designed for young children (ages 3–8) who are learning to identify animals. Parents set up the profile using a quick maths gate to keep the experience safe and supervised, so you can feel confident your child is playing in a protected environment.

## No Download Needed

You can play Wild Guess & Match entirely in your browser — there is nothing to install. Once you load the game for the first time, it also works offline as a Progressive Web App (PWA), so the fun doesn't stop if your connection drops. Wild Guess & Match works on phones, tablets, and desktops.
