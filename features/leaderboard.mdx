The Daily Leaderboard shows today's top 10 players ranked by their total score. It resets every day at midnight UTC, giving everyone a fresh chance to climb to the top.

## Reading the Leaderboard

Each row on the leaderboard shows you the following information at a glance:

- **Rank badge** — the top three spots wear special colours: 🥇 1st place is **gold**, 🥈 2nd place is **silver**, and 🥉 3rd place is **bronze**. All other ranks display a plain white badge.
- **Player avatar and nickname** — every player is represented by an animal avatar alongside their chosen nickname.
- **Score** — the number of correct game actions that player has submitted today, displayed in blue as **pts**.

If **you** are in the top 10, your row is highlighted in **orange** so you can spot yourself immediately. If you are ranked 11th or lower, the top 10 list shows without your entry — but scroll down to find a separate **Your Rank** section below the list, which shows your current position and score in the same orange-highlighted style.

## How Scores Are Counted

Every successful game action adds **1 point** to your daily score:

- A **correct guess** in the Guess Game counts as 1 point.
- A **matched pair** found in the Match Game counts as 1 point.

Coins you earn during play do **not** affect your leaderboard rank. Only the total number of correct guesses and matched pairs determines where you appear on the board.

## Daily Reset

Look at the banner at the top of the leaderboard — it shows a countdown in **hours and minutes** until the next UTC midnight reset. When that timer hits zero, all scores and ranks return to zero for every player, and a brand-new day of competition begins.

<Note>
  The leaderboard updates in real time. Tap the page to refresh if you want to see the latest rankings.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Play in the Guess Game and Match Game to accumulate points quickly. More rounds = higher score!
</Tip>
