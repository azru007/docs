Every new player needs their own profile before they can start learning and earning coins. Before you get to pick your nickname and avatar, a parent or guardian must complete a quick maths check — this is a safety feature that makes sure a grown-up is present when a new account is created.

## Setting Up Your Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete the Parent Check">
    The first screen you see is the **Ask an Adult** gate. Hand the device to a grown-up — they will see a simple addition question, for example:

    > **What is 5 + 7?**

    The numbers change each time, always between 2 and 9, so the question is easy for an adult but tricky for very young children. The grown-up types their answer and taps **Confirm**.

    If the answer is wrong, a brand-new question is generated automatically and they can try again straight away.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a Nickname">
    Once the parent check passes, you move to the **Choose Your Profile** screen. Type your nickname into the box — it can be anything you like, up to **15 characters** long.

    Your nickname appears on the leaderboard and inside the game, so pick something you love!
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick an Avatar">
    Below the nickname box you will find a grid of **6 animal avatars** to choose from: **Lion**, **Cow**, **Monkey**, **Elephant**, **Rabbit**, and **Frog**.

    Tap any animal to select it. The chosen avatar gets a bright yellow highlighted border so you always know which one is active.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap Let's Play!">
    When you are happy with your nickname and avatar, tap the big green **Let's Play!** button. Your profile is created instantly and the game loads straight away — no waiting around!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your profile is saved automatically the moment you tap **Let's Play!** You never need to create it again. Every time you come back to Wild Guess & Match, the game remembers your nickname, your chosen avatar, your coin balance, and whether you have already unlocked the Drawing Studio.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Parents: help your child pick a nickname that is fun and age-appropriate — something like **"StarLion"** or **"JungleKid"** works great and keeps things safe if they ever make it onto the leaderboard.
</Tip>

## Changing Your Profile

When you return to Wild Guess & Match, your saved profile loads automatically — you will go straight into the game without seeing the setup screens again.

If you want to start completely fresh (for example, to create a profile for a different child), a parent can **clear the browser's site data** for Wild Guess & Match. You can usually find this option in your browser under **Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data**, then choosing to clear data only for this site.

Clearing site data resets everything: the profile, the nickname, the avatar, and the coin balance. The parent maths check will appear again the next time someone visits the game.
