Wild Guess & Match is a browser-based learning game that makes animal recognition fun for kids. Play the **Guess Game** to identify animals from pictures, challenge your memory in the **Match Game**, unleash creativity in the **Drawing Studio**, and compete with friends on the **Daily Leaderboard** — all while earning coins for every correct answer.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask a grown-up to set up your profile">
    A parent or guardian answers a simple maths question to unlock the profile setup screen. Then choose a nickname (up to 15 characters) and pick a cute animal avatar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play games to earn coins">
    Jump into the **Guess Game** or **Match Game**. Every correct answer earns coins — build streaks in the Guess Game for bonus rewards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlock the Drawing Studio">
    Once you have collected 50 coins, spend them to open the Drawing Studio. Paint freely, stamp animals, and save your masterpieces to your personal gallery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Climb the leaderboard">
    Your scores count toward the **Daily Leaderboard**, which resets every day at midnight UTC. See how you rank against other players!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Wild Guess & Match works on phones, tablets, and desktop computers — no app download needed. It also works offline once loaded, thanks to PWA support.
</Note>
